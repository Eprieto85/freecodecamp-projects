Classic Spaghetti Bolognese

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🍳 Cook: 30 min 🍽 Serves: 4

A hearty Italian meat sauce served over perfectly cooked spaghetti. Simple, comforting, and absolutely delicious — perfect for weeknight dinners.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves to garnish
  • Parmesan cheese, grated (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion for about 5 minutes until translucent.
  2. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 more minute, stirring constantly.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, add oregano, stir well, and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes.
  5. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain.
  6. Serve the Bolognese sauce over the spaghetti. Garnish with fresh basil and Parmesan if desired.
